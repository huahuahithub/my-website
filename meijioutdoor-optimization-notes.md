# Meiji Outdoor Website Optimization Notes

## Current Position

The website can support outdoor knives, BBQ tools, and outdoor carry bags, but the product structure should be presented as a focused outdoor tools and gear manufacturer rather than a broad general outdoor store.

## Key Issues To Improve

1. The homepage positioning is still knife-led, while the navigation already includes many future categories such as BBQ Tools, Kitchen Tools, Shovel, Axe, Titanium Tools, and Survival Kit.
2. The homepage currently has an empty H1, which weakens SEO and makes the page topic less clear to search engines.
3. Some categories, including BBQ Tools, are visible but have no related products, which can reduce buyer trust.
4. Product titles are too long and keyword-stuffed; they should be shorter, clearer, and more buyer-friendly.
5. Product pages need stronger B2B information, including MOQ, lead time, packaging, logo customization, sample policy, certifications, and inquiry guidance.
6. The factory story needs more trust signals, including production photos, QC process, certificates, sample room, packaging area, and team images.
7. Knife products need compliance-sensitive wording for the US, UK, and EU markets, especially around automatic knives, switchblades, self-defense wording, age restrictions, and import rules.
8. BBQ tools should be positioned under outdoor cooking or camp cooking, not as a generic kitchen category.
9. Outdoor bags should start as tool rolls, knife storage bags, EDC pouches, BBQ tool bags, and camping gear organizers before expanding into general backpacks.
10. Category pages need SEO copy, buyer-focused introductions, and internal links instead of only product grids.

## Recommended Category Direction

- Outdoor Knives
- BBQ & Camp Cooking
- Outdoor Bags & Carry
- OEM Custom Manufacturing
- Factory & Quality Control
- Contact / Business Inquiry

## Recommended Short-Term Priority

1. Make the knife category look more professional and export-ready.
2. Hide or delay empty categories until products and content are ready.
3. Build one professional English landing page for outdoor knives and OEM customization.
4. Add BBQ tools later as a related outdoor cooking line.
5. Add bags as storage and carry accessories for knives, BBQ tools, and camping gear.
